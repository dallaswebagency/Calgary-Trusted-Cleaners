FAQ

Common questions

How much does cleaning cost?

It depends on property size, cleaning type and frequency. Request a custom quote. Recurring cleanings are more cost-effective than one-time or deep cleans.

What does a standard cleaning include?

Dusting, vacuuming, mopping, kitchen and bathroom cleaning and surface wipe-downs. Deep cleaning, appliance interiors and move-in/move-out cleaning are add-ons.

Do I need to provide supplies?

No. Our cleaners bring professional-grade supplies and equipment. Specific product requests can often be accommodated.

How long does a cleaning take?

Most residential cleanings take 2 to 5 hours. Larger homes and deep cleans take longer.

Do I need to be home?

No. Many clients give us secure access and have their cleaning done while they are away.

Do you clean commercial spaces?

Yes. Offices, retail, restaurants and other facilities, customized to your business needs and compliance requirements.
