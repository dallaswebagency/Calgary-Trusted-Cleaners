Our story

Why we started

Our founders previously owned and ran three large health clubs, where keeping the facilities clean and welcoming was a daily priority. They were often unhappy with the cleaning services they hired, which overpromised and underdelivered.

So they started Calgary Trusted Cleaners to provide the exceptional cleaning services they always wished they had.
